      Scribble together (cubby.draw)

      Hold Alt and the pointer becomes a puck; hold and drag to draw. Strokes are captured as vectors and sent as whole paths, so a scribble is a handful of events rather than hundreds. Sign in and open a second window to share; nothing is persisted.
